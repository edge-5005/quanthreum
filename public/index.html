Why Quanthreum is Immune to Quantum Attacks

Traditional ECDSA (secp256k1) used in Bitcoin and Ethereum will collapse once 2,000+ qubit quantum computers arrive. Quanthreum solves this at the protocol layer.

Vulnerable to Shor's Algorithm

Standard Ethereum (ECDSA)

  • Entropy / Seed: Standard 128 / 256 bits (12 or 24 words)
  • Signature Algorithm: SECP256k1 Elliptic Curve
  • Shor's Algorithm: Breaks discrete logarithm in polynomial time $O((\log N)^3)$.
  • Private Key Exposure: Public key on-chain allows quantum computers to instantly derive the private key.
  • Long-Term Longevity: High risk of obsolescence within the decade.
512-bit Post-Quantum Secure

Quanthreum (W-OTS+ over SHA3)

  • Entropy / Seed: 512-bit Quantum Entropy (48 words)
  • Signature Algorithm: Winternitz W-OTS+ (NIST PQC Standard)
  • Shor's Algorithm: Completely ineffective — hash chains lack abelian group structure!
  • Grover's Attack: SHA3-256 leaves $2^{128}$ quantum operations (physically unbreakable).
  • Address Format: Quantum Checksummed 0xQTH_... format.
